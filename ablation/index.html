Research user study

Comparing three video-editing setups

You'll watch a short input video together with a one-sentence edit request, then rate three edited versions and tell us which one follows the request best.

How it works

  1. 1 Read the edit request and watch the original input video.
  2. 2 Watch Option A, Option B, and Option C — three edited versions, shown in random order.
  3. 3
    For each option, rate two things on a scale of 1–5:
    • ✓ Edit success. How well does it perform the requested edit — from not at all (1) to completely (5)?
    • ✨ Motion naturalness. How natural does the motion look — from very unnatural (1) to very natural (5)?
  4. 4
    Rank the three from best to worst — tap them in the order you prefer.

About the audio: this study is only about motion editing. Sound is included purely for reference/context — please don't judge or score a video based on its audio or sound quality.

There are 16 scenarios. It takes about 20–30 minutes.

Your privacy

Your responses are completely anonymous — we do not collect your name, email, or any other personal information. We only record your choices on the video samples shown, which will be combined with everyone else's responses into aggregate statistics.
